# v42 changes (2026-10-13)

Base v41. Copy and small responsive fixes only. No migration, no scoring, clue-cost, unlock, streak or reward-rule change.

## 1. Shorter copy
Shortened (meaning kept): home WhatsApp note, agency story, project support, sign-in notes (host, email-only, guest), language notices, Membership (intro, launch prices, sprint definition, price and renewal terms), Settings language note, ChatGPT companion page, rewards help ("How do I collect rewards?", now three short paragraphs), streak and title rules (all pinned phrases kept), link hint on practical tasks, two duplicate link sentences in lib/practical/marketing.json and storytelling.json.
Kept as written: mission questions and steps, safety lines, pricing conditions, refund/legal pages, account and privacy disclosures, clue costs.

## 2. Points balance always visible
New components/whatslearn/points-chip.tsx, shown under the header on /learn (sticky on screens up to 820px):
- loading: "Points: loading…"
- signed in: "N points" from the server balance (nothing hard-coded)
- load failed: "Points could not load." plus Retry (never a silent 0)
- guest: "N guest points · this browser only" (no welcome credit implied)
The XP line in Your Collection also shows the balance or its status. v41 behaviour is unchanged: 50 granted once per account on the server, "Welcome! 50 points added" shown once, +X and clue deduction messages with new balance.

## 3. Small responsive fixes (app/globals.css, appended)
- Header links wrap at 800px and below (they were clipped, "Explore WhatsLearn").
- Workspace tab row wraps at 520px and below ("Membership" was clipped at 360px).
