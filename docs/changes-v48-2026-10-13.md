# Changes in v48 (2026-10-13), base v47

Mascot only. Files: `components/whatslearn/mascot.tsx`, the mascot block of `app/globals.css`, `tests/mascot-poses.cjs`, docs.

- **Character only:** every caption, label and speech bubble (added in v45) is removed. The drawing has no text. The only text left in the mascot is the visually hidden live region that announces a recorded clue, a saved mission and a cleared mini challenge to screen readers.
- **Idle loop:** unchanged from v47 and seamless: every idle keyframe starts and ends at the same value, so the loop shows no jump.
- **Hover (mouse):** the figure reacts once, then eases back into the idle loop. The reaction rotates: wave (the free arm swings up and waves), small excited bounce (squash and hop), blink with a nod. Poses with no free arm (My Journey, mini challenge) rotate bounce and blink. Each reaction ends by itself after about 1.5 s; its keyframes start and end at rest, so there is no snap.
- **Tap (touch and pen):** the same brief reaction on tap; the idle loop continues.
- **Rules kept:** a thumbs-up or cheer is never interrupted by hover or tap. Thumbs-up only after a recorded clue; jump and cheer only after a confirmed mission save or a cleared mini challenge; nothing on failures.
- **Decorative:** `aria-hidden`, no tab stop, no button role, no click handler. Pointer events only on the figure, which stands in its own space, so no button, link or field is blocked.
- **Reduced motion:** still pose; hover and tap change nothing.
- No audio, no new dependency. Layout, colors, content, behavior unchanged. The spot on each page is unchanged.
