# v32 (12 October 2026): course missions, daily limit and mission points (fuller v31 spec)

> **Superseded in v35:** the one-new-mission-a-day limit now uses the India (Asia/Kolkata) calendar day for everyone, signed in or guest. Where this note mentions the learner's local day or a time zone sent by the browser, read `docs/changes-v35-2026-10-12.md` instead.

v32 is v31 plus the fuller spec: difficulty bands, the learner's local calendar day, Hindi and Kannada answers, and a test that the interface is unchanged. Everything below applies to v32. The v31 ZIP is untouched.

Built from v30. The look, layout, navigation and styling are unchanged. Only course content, scoring and enforcement logic changed, and a few lines of text in existing places.

## Courses and missions
- Four existing courses: Marketing (`AI Digital Marketing`), Full Stack Development (`AI Full-Stack Development`), Filmmaking (`AI Filmmaking`) and Storytelling (`AI-powered Visual Storytelling`). The names and track ids did not change.
- **10 missions per course (40 in total).** The 10 earlier missions are kept. 30 new ones are in `lib/course-missions.ts`: marketing m4 to m10, development d3 to d10, filmmaking f4 to f10, storytelling v3 to v10.
- **Exactly two scored questions per mission:** the understanding question (`question`, `options`, `answer`) and the practical "try it" task (`task`). The 30-second side quest (`mini`) is the clue, not a third question. Tone: college and early-career, with Indian settings and rupee figures.
- Mini puzzles are in `courseMini` and are merged in `lib/play.ts`. Kannada and Hindi fall back to English for the 30 new missions until a human translates them.

## How the daily limit works
- A learner can **complete one new mission a day, across all four courses**. They choose the course. The day is the **learner's own calendar day**: the browser sends its time zone name (`timeZone`, for example `Asia/Kolkata`) and the server converts its own clock to that zone (`lib/local-day.ts`). The browser cannot send a date, a score or points; those are ignored. A missing or unknown zone falls back to India time. Limit: the learner's device chooses the zone, so someone who deliberately changes their device time zone could claim two days in a row. Practice-day counts for rewards still use India time as before.
- Signed in: the database enforces it. Table `mission_scores` has a primary key on (user, mission) and a unique index on (user, day) (`drizzle/0007_mission_points.sql`). The score and the saved work are written in one all-or-nothing batch, so a refresh, a double tap, two tabs or six simultaneous requests can award points only once.
- A second new mission on the same day is refused with a message and **nothing is saved**, so the mission stays open for tomorrow.
- Revisiting a mission is allowed any time. It saves as practice and shows "points are not awarded twice". Work saved before v31 counts as practice and does not use up the day.
- Guests: kept in this browser only (`lib/guest-points.ts`, key `whatslearn-guest-points-v1`). The limit and points do not follow the learner to another device or browser, clearing site data resets them, and a technical user could edit them. The page says so. Sign in to keep them with the account.

## Difficulty bands
Missions 1 and 2 of each course are beginner, 3 to 7 intermediate and 8 to 10 advanced (`level` on each mission, set by position in `lib/missions.ts`). The advanced missions ask for the longest answers. Whether a scenario truly feels beginner or advanced is an editorial call that a course owner should review.

## Hindi and Kannada
Answers written mostly in another script are checked against the mission's words in that language (`lib/course-terms.ts`, 14 Hindi and 14 Kannada words per new mission, drafted by an AI assistant and **not reviewed by a native speaker**). Other supported languages are only checked for the 10 earlier missions. Relevance, copying and filler checks still apply; the English-only reasoning check is skipped for non-Latin answers.

## Progress, streaks and missed days
Nothing is deleted when a day is missed. A scored mission always writes its practice-day event, even when clues were used or the answer was revised, so it counts toward practice days. There is no separate streak counter in the app; the practice-day count is what exists.

## The interface is unchanged
`tests/mission-points.cjs` compares the style sheets, the class names and element structure of the four changed files, the component list and the page list with a fingerprint of v30 (`tests/fixtures/ui-v30-baseline.json`). It will fail if a future change alters them on purpose; regenerate the fingerprint with `node tests/fixtures/ui-fingerprint.cjs` only for an approved UI change. New messages appear inside existing paragraphs and status areas.

## How scoring works
- Up to **100 points**: 25 for the understanding question (right answer) plus up to 75 for the practical task. Practical points follow the quick writing check: strong 75, good 60, needs work 45, getting started 30.
- Points are given **only after both questions are answered** and the practical answer passes the relevance checks. The saved work and the points are written together.
- **Clues:** the side-quest clue, "Show an example" and "Give me a hint" each cost **5 points once**, at most **10 per mission**. The total never goes below zero. The deduction is shown in the result ("90 points: understanding 25 + practical 75 − clues 10"). Clues are recorded in `mission_clues` (signed in) or this device (guest) and write no practice-day data, so **a clue cannot break a streak or the practice-day count**.
- "Explain simply" and the live AI question box are not clues.

## Relevance checks (`lib/mission-score.ts`, `lib/writing-check.ts`)
Refused with guidance and a chance to try again, with nothing saved: empty, `abcd`-style and filler text, logs, answers that copy the mission's example or lesson (5-word phrase overlap, so light rewording is caught), answers with too few of this mission's own ideas, answers written for another course, and, where the task says "explain" or "why", answers with no stated reason. Paste is never blocked, and nothing here claims to detect plagiarism or AI-written text. It cannot prove who wrote an answer.
The check is a quick rule-based check on this site. It is labelled that way everywhere and is not an AI review. The "AI review" label still appears only for a real, complete AI reply.
Known limit: a few neighbouring missions (for example two pitch missions) share words, so an answer for one may pass the other. Measured on the sample answers: all 40 relevant samples pass, 0 of 160 copied texts pass, and 3 of 1,200 cross-course answers pass.
