# Agency starter and language selection — 6 October 2026

The owner approved a hero that gives learners a small win before personalisation and asked to replace the coffee example with a short story about a content creation agency.

The hero now opens with “Play a little. Build a real skill.” and a playable story: a Bengaluru agency receives a Friday-launch brief on Monday. The learner clarifies the audience and desired action, then makes a short piece. Four distinct lanes lead to a campaign hook, a client-approval flow, a launch-film moment or a three-frame visual story. The next links map to m1, d1, f1 and v1 respectively. These are introductory practice pieces, not replacements for the existing mission tasks or assessments.

The abstract sculpture and setup-first hero button are replaced by the real challenge. The primary button focuses the decision; personalisation follows saving. Returning visitors with saved work see Continue my journey. The logo, From Chat to Change, company identity, contact details, prices, integrations and access remain unchanged.

## Language scope

English is the default. The dropdown includes English and all 22 Eighth Schedule languages, using native-script names and stable locale codes. The list is checked against the Ministry of Home Affairs source: https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/EighthSchedule_19052017.pdf (reviewed 6 October 2026). This covers the scheduled-language set; it is not a claim to cover every language or dialect spoken in India.

The starter story, decision, guidance and four creation prompts are authored in English, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu. These are authored translations, not a claim of native-speaker review. The remaining nine scheduled-language choices are selectable and retain the learner's preference, with an explicit translation-pending notice and English starter guidance. Responses can be written in the chosen language.

Full source missions remain in English, Kannada and Hindi. Other preferences use labelled English source guidance; the existing ChatGPT companion is instructed to coach in the selected language and identify conversational translations. The website does not call a new translation service or promise that every screen has a complete translation.

The chosen language persists locally and is carried through starter links, the path finder and learning journey. Explicitly saved profiles accept all 23 locale choices on the website and through the existing companion. Account profile saving still requires authentication. Content language is identified separately from preferred language so an English fallback is not presented as a translated source lesson. Urdu uses right-to-left story layout and response fields use automatic writing direction.

## Work and rewards

Agency starter pieces have a separate device-local key. Old café work is retained and is not re-labelled or silently imported. Track changes retain unsaved drafts during the current visit. The starter appreciation is local; full mission coins and XP retain their existing rules and are not awarded for a warmup. No additional learner data is submitted to the team.

## Validation

Check language persistence, explicit fallback, separate mission routes, valid starter submissions and preservation of old café work. Run TypeScript and the production build before publication, preserving the existing public audience.
