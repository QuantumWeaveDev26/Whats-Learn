# Changes in v45 (2026-10-13), base v44

**Mascot revised.** v44 drew the figure lying along the top border of the main card (a wide, short strip with a negative margin), so it read as a cropped decoration. It is replaced.

- New figure: upright stick figure with glasses and a simple smile, same line style, now with a small green pin on the shirt. It stands in the page flow beside the page's heading or card, with space around it, and never touches a card border or covers text or controls. A short line sits beside it in a speech bubble (body-size text, no font reduced).
- One pose per page (see the handoff checklist). The pose is chosen from the page, and from the open dialog on My Journey.
- Reactions are unchanged in rule and one added: thumbs-up only after a clue is recorded; jump and cheer only after a mission save is accepted, and now also after a mini challenge is cleared (guest: when the local check passes; signed in: when the server says passed). Failed answers, failed clue requests and failed saves change nothing.
- Lightweight inline SVG and CSS. No audio. With reduced motion every pose is a still drawing; the live-region text still announces thumbs-up, saved and cleared.
- Placement: home inside the hero text, My Journey under the page heading, mission and mini challenge at the top of the open dialog, other pages above their main card.
- No change to scoring, points, checkpoints, copy, routes or the rest of the design.
