# Changes in v46 (2026-10-13), base v45

Existing parts inspected first: the shared mascot (`components/whatslearn/mascot.tsx`, mounted in `app/layout.tsx`), the cards (`.public-path`, `.path-card`, `.price-card`, `.membership-card`, `.rhythm-card`, `.strategy-card`, `.portfolio-card`, `.mission-card`, `.sprint-stop`, `.checkpoint-stop`, `.pulse-card`) and the sections (`main section`, `.section-pad`). The home page already revealed its headings with `will-arrive`; that is left as it was.

- **Card hover and press** (`app/motion.css`, new): with a mouse, cards lift 2px with a soft shadow over 0.2s. The home path cards lift 3px (they lifted 8px before, which was too jumpy). Only transform and shadow change, so size, neighbours and page height stay put. Pressing a card (touch or mouse) shows a small pressed state, so touch does not rely on hover.
- **Keyboard focus:** when anything inside a card has keyboard focus, the card gets a white-and-green ring. Mouse clicks do not leave a ring.
- **Scroll reveal** (`components/whatslearn/reveal.tsx`, new, mounted in the layout): blocks that start below the first screen fade in and rise 14px as they scroll into view, one after another (70 ms steps, at most 5). Nothing on the first screen is hidden, nothing is hidden with scripting off or with reduced motion, classes are removed after the reveal, and there is no scroll handling, parallax or scroll hijacking.
- **Reduced motion:** no transitions on cards, no hover movement, no reveal classes; everything is visible at once.
- **Mascot placement fix:** on Membership (desktop) the mascot became a cell of the plans grid and pushed the plan cards sideways. It now stands before the whole group whenever its anchor sits in a grid or a flex row.
- **Sticky and floating elements:** the mobile points bar (about 50 px) is the only sticky bar; the page scroll padding is 95 px, so jumps to a heading are not covered. The points message is a short pop-up at the bottom of the screen, as before.
- Tests: `tests/motion.cjs` (3 tests); `tests/mission-points.cjs` now lists `reveal.tsx` as an added component. No new dependency.
- Unchanged: mission progression, points, clues, streaks, mini challenges, sign-in, copy, the ChatGPT link, `app/globals.css`.
