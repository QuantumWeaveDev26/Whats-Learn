# WhatsLearn v22 changes (10 October 2026)

Built from the v21 ZIP. Fixes "the site accepts anything and gives one star".

**Cause.** Stars never read the writing: 1 star for any saved answer of 40+ characters with the quiz right, a 2nd only when the learner ticked 4 self-review boxes, a 3rd only for a revision. The AI review (when switched on) did not affect stars. This happened with or without an AI key.

**Fix.**
- `lib/writing-check.ts`: a basic writing check that needs no AI key. It reads the actual text: word count against the task's target (e.g. 40–80 words), whether it mentions the mission's ideas (in any lesson language), sentence structure and word variety, and signs of copying the example or filler. It gives a band (Strong, Good, On the way, Just getting started), a star rating and one concrete tip. It is clearly labelled as a basic check.
- Empty, filler, off-topic or copied-example answers cannot be saved (on the device and on the server), with the reason shown.
- Stars now follow the writing: 1 for a saved creation, 2 when the check rates it Good, 3 when it is Strong or improved in a revision. Ticking boxes alone no longer raises stars.
- Review step: new "Quick writing check" card with live stars, word count and tip; it says that an AI key (OpenAI or Claude) enables a detailed review.
- AI review: shows an "AI rating" in stars from how many checklist points the AI found met. If the AI call fails, a clear message is shown and no score is given.
- Tests: `tests/writing-check.cjs` (good, weak, empty, filler, copied, off-topic and Hindi answers; stars; server-side blocking; AI rating and failure). `tests/play.test.ts` updated for writing-based stars.
