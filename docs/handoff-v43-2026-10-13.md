## Choices I made
- **Checkpoint reward:** a badge, not points (you said not to invent point amounts). One row per learner, course and position.
- **The finale after mission 10** earns a badge and gates nothing (there is no mission 11).
- **Task order:** Task 1: Understand now sits before Task 2: Try it in the Make step. The Review step no longer holds the question.
- **Mini challenges are English only** for now.
- **Existing learners:** a learner who has done mission 2 but not 3 must clear checkpoint 2 before mission 3. A mission already completed stays open.
