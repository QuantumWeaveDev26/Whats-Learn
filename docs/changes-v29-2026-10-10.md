## 2. Answer review
- AI review (`lib/ai-feedback.ts` `reviewWork`) now runs the quick writing check first. Meaningless text such as "abcd", filler, copied examples, off-topic text and pasted logs or code are refused with the check's advice and status 422. No AI call is made and the daily allowance is not used. This also covers WhatsApp replies.
- An AI reply is shown as an "AI review" only when it is a complete review. Provider errors, empty replies and replies that are not a review are reported as failures, with nothing reviewed or rated and no allowance used.
- The AI review button stays disabled until the quick check passes, and says why.
- The home starter now accepts a click on "Save my first piece" for any text and explains what to write, instead of a disabled button.
- The quick check card no longer tells learners about AI keys; it says it is "not an AI review".
- Test: `tests/review-gate.cjs` (abcd, filler and the Wrangler log refused on the starter, on save and before AI review; a relevant answer saves and gets an AI review; five AI failure cases).

## 3. Payments
- The existing integration uses Razorpay Standard Checkout with no payment-method limits, so Razorpay shows every method enabled on the account. Skill Sprint is a one-time order (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets as enabled). Plus, Pro and Teams are subscriptions, where Razorpay offers cards, UPI Autopay and e-mandate (net banking or debit card) only if Subscriptions and those methods are activated on the account.
- Test checkout stays owner-only, `/billing/setup` stays owner-only, and live payments and public checkout stay off by default (no change from v28).
- Wording: checkout states the methods honestly in test and live mode. The "coming soon" lines on the home FAQ, the learning page and the companion page now say paid checkout is not open yet and nothing is charged.
- Access is granted only after the Razorpay signature, payment and order or subscription status are verified on the server (no change).
