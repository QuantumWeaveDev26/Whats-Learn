## 1. Wrong-course answers (the 3 of 1,200)
The three cases were: a dialogue answer written for filmmaking f4 accepted in marketing m4 (both use quoted questions), and a storytelling pitch (v9) accepted in filmmaking f8 and f9. Two causes: the "another course fits better" rule only fired when another course led by double, and no check compared against missions in other courses.
Fix (`lib/mission-score.ts`): an answer is refused as the wrong course when another course's words lead this course's by 2 or more, or when a mission in another course matches the answer much better than this mission does (3 more mission words and twice as many). Result on the sample answers: **0 of 1,200** cross-course answers pass (was 3); all 40 correct answers still pass; shortened correct answers are refused for the wrong-course reason in no case (same results as v32). Missions in the same course intentionally share terms and are not treated as the wrong course.
Regression cases (`tests/mission-points.cjs`): m4/f4, f8/v9, f9/v9 and their reverses, plus f10/v9, m10/v10, d4/m7, m5/d8, v6/m7, v7/m8, each with a check that the right mission still accepts its own answer. This is still a rule-based check. It can be fooled and does not detect plagiarism or AI-written text.

## 3. Streak and practice preference
- **Streak: added as text only.** The rewards card's XP line ("0 practice XP · 0 creations") now ends with "· 3-day streak" when the learner has practised on 2 or more consecutive India-time days, or "· best streak N" after a break. A missed day resets only the current run; practice days, points and saved work are never reduced. Checked in a browser at desktop and 390 px width: the line stays on one row and the card height does not change. No new element, class, style or screen.
- **Practice preference: left out.** Settings already has "How do you start?" (mini challenge, story, worked example) and a mode switch, saved on the device. Visual, hands-on and challenge-based options would need new radio options in Settings and different behaviour per mission step, which means a layout and control change. Not added.
- A **daily goal ring and learning-style quiz** also need new screens or components and were left out.
