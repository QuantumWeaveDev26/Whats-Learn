# v43 changes (2026-10-13)

Base v42. The green-and-white design, layout and navigation are unchanged. No change to scoring, clue costs, the India-time daily limit, the 50 starting points, the streak and title rules, membership or the ChatGPT companion link.

## 1. Mascot (every page)
One shared component, `components/whatslearn/mascot.tsx`, mounted once in `app/layout.tsx`. It is a small SVG and CSS stick figure (no video, no animation library, no audio), drawn from the stick-figure reference: round head, big round glasses, thin limbs.
- **Idle:** lies along the top border of the page's main challenge card and whistles now and then (small mouth change and two music notes, one 10-second cycle, not constant).
- **Clue revealed:** a short thumbs-up, then idle. Fired only after the clue was recorded (server or guest), never on click, never when recording fails.
- **Mission saved:** a short jump and cheer, then idle. Fired only after the save succeeded (a refused save such as the daily limit does not cheer).
- **Where it sits:** on the first `.mini-quest` card (home, My Journey), a marked card (Find my path, Membership, My skill paths, My Creations), the first account card (Settings, Companion, sign-in, sign-up and other account pages) or the policy text (Privacy, Terms, Refunds). In an open lesson or mini-challenge dialog it moves into the dialog. Pages with no card get a small spot at the end of the page. It takes its own space, so it never covers text, buttons or fields.
- **Accessibility:** the drawing is `aria-hidden`; "Clue revealed. Thumbs up." and "Mission saved. Well done." go to a polite live region.
- **Reduced motion:** a still drawing. No animation names are applied; a clue or save swaps to a still pose and the text announcement still plays.

## 2. Mini challenges (checkpoints)
- After missions 2, 4, 6 and 8 of each of the four courses, and a course finale after mission 10: 20 checkpoints in `lib/checkpoints.ts`, each a short, course-specific practical scenario with two questions (three options each). Both must be right.
- Not a mission: no mission score, no points, no ledger row, and it does not use the one-new-mission-a-day allowance.
- **Reward (my default):** a badge, one per learner, course and position. No new point amounts. The badge is a row in the new table `checkpoint_clears`, whose key refuses a second row, so retries and double taps cannot duplicate it.
- **Lock:** missions 3, 5, 7 and 9 stay locked until the checkpoint before them is cleared, on the server (`saveMissionWork`, `/api/work`) and for guests on this device. A completed mission always stays open to practise. The finale gates nothing.
- **Retry:** a wrong answer says "That answer is not correct.", gives the reason and invites another try. Picks and progress are kept.
- **Explained:** a ready banner on My Journey, a row of five mini challenges per course, a plain "Locked · clear the mini challenge after mission N" on the stage, and a locked-mission dialog with a "Start the mini challenge" button.
- **Guests:** badges kept in this browser only (`whatslearn-guest-checkpoints-v1`), labelled as such.
- **Server:** `POST /api/checkpoint` (sign-in required, strict body, answers checked on the server, the mission before it must be completed). `GET /api/work` also returns the cleared keys. Migration `0010_checkpoint_clears.sql`.
- Mini challenges are English only for now (Kannada and Hindi learners see English).

## 3. Task labels
Each mission now shows **Task 1: Understand** (the knowledge question) first and **Task 2: Try it** (the practical task) second, in the Make step. "Review my work" opens once Task 1 is correct and Task 2 is complete. Points are unchanged (25 + 75). The Review step no longer repeats the question.

## 4. Copy
Rewards help is split into short paragraphs (and now names the tasks and mini challenges). The v42 copy cuts carry forward. Only English strings were edited, so translated packs can still be longer than the English.

## 5. Small responsive fix
Nothing new beyond the mascot and checkpoint styles, which were checked at 360, 390 and 1280 px.
